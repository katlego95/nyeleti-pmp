What is PMP?

PMP — Project Management Professional — is the global standard certification for project leaders. Issued by PMI (Project Management Institute), it's recognised across IT, healthcare, finance, construction, and government in 200+ countries.

For someone pivoting from software engineering toward technical leadership, PMP signals you can ship complex work through cross-functional teams under real constraints — schedule, scope, budget, people. It's the credential hiring managers look for on Senior PM, Programme Manager, and Engineering Manager specs.

The exam itself is 180 questions in 3h50min. Behind it: 36 months of project leadership experience and 35 hours of formal training. It's earned, not granted.
